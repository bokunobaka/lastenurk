# Printsess Frida päev

Estonian text for a 5-year-old reader: short sentences, sound words (*viuh, hopp, amps*) that are fun to read aloud.
The source of truth used by the web book and video is `web/story.js`; edit there, then update this file.

| # | Eesti keeles | English (for reference) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Printsess Frida päev** | Princess Frida's Day |
| 1 | Hommikul paistis päike aknast sisse. Printsess Frida ärkas üles, sirutas käsi ja haigutas: „Tere hommikust!” | In the morning the sun shone through the window. Princess Frida woke up, stretched her arms and yawned: "Good morning!" |
| 2 | Frida läks vannituppa ja pesi hambaid. Üleval ja all, vasakul ja paremal – kuni hambad särasid! | Frida went to the bathroom and brushed her teeth. Top and bottom, left and right – until her teeth sparkled! |
| 3 | Siis pani Frida selga oma lemmikkleidi – roosa, lilla ja kollase. Pähe pani ta krooni, millel oli kolm säravat kalliskivi. | Then Frida put on her favourite dress – pink, purple and yellow. On her head she put her crown with three shining jewels. |
| 4 | Frida istus pehmesse tugitooli ja luges raamatuid. Raamatutes olid lohed, haldjad ja üks väga naljakas konn. | Frida sat in a soft armchair and read books. In the books there were dragons, fairies and one very funny frog. |
| 5 | Õuest kostis naeru. Seal ootasid Fridat tema sõbrad: Mia, Lenna, Eliise ja Kertu. „Tule mängima!” hüüdsid nad. | Laughter came from outside. Her friends Mia, Lenna, Eliise and Kertu were waiting. "Come and play!" they called. |
| 6 | Printsessid mängisid palli. Pall lendas kõrgele-kõrgele ja kõik jooksid palli järele. Hopp! | The princesses played ball. The ball flew high, high up and everyone ran after it. Hop! |
| 7 | Siis jooksid nad suurele mänguväljakule. Seal oli väga kõrge liumägi! Üksteise järel lasid printsessid alla: viuh, viuh, viuh, viuh, viuh! | Then they ran to the big playground. There was a very tall slide! One after another the princesses slid down: whoosh ×5! |
| 8 | Järgmisena läksid nad hüppelossi. Seal sai hüpata, roomata läbi tunnelite ja lasta liumäest alla. Hopp-hopp-hopp! | Next they went to the bouncy castle. You could jump, crawl through tunnels and go down the slide. Bounce-bounce-bounce! |
| 9 | Aias kasvas suur ploomipuu. Printsessid ronisid puu otsa ja korjasid küpseid lillasid ploome. Amps! Kui magusad! | A big plum tree grew in the garden. The princesses climbed up and picked ripe purple plums. Chomp! So sweet! |
| 10 | Varsti oli lõunaaeg. Printsessid lehvitasid üksteisele: „Head aega! Homme mängime jälle!” Ja kõik läksid koju. | Soon it was lunchtime. The princesses waved: "Goodbye! Let's play again tomorrow!" And everyone went home. |
| 11 | Kodus ootas Fridat soe lõunasöök. Frida sõi kõhu täis ja ütles: „Aitäh, oli väga maitsev!” | A warm lunch was waiting for Frida at home. She ate until she was full and said: "Thank you, it was delicious!" |
| 12 | Enne lõunauinakut vahetas Frida riided. Kleit läks kappi, selga tuli pehme pidžaama ja kroon jäi öökapile puhkama. | Before her nap Frida changed clothes. The dress went into the wardrobe, soft pyjamas went on, and the crown rested on the nightstand. |
| 13 | Frida pugis teki alla ja sulges silmad. Ka Mia, Lenna, Eliise ja Kertu magasid oma kodudes. Magusat und, printsessid! | Frida snuggled under the blanket and closed her eyes. Mia, Lenna, Eliise and Kertu slept in their homes too. Sweet dreams, princesses! |
| 14 | Pärast uinakut olid printsessid jälle reipad. Nad panid kleidid selga, kroonid pähe ja võtsid kaasa oma võlukepid. | After the nap the princesses were full of energy again. They put on their dresses and crowns and took their magic wands. |
| 15 | Aia taga seisis nende ühine suur lohe. Ta oli liikumatu nagu kivikuju. Printsessid tõstsid võlukepid ja hüüdsid: „Üks, kaks, kolm – ärka üles, lohe!” | Behind the garden stood their shared big dragon, still as a stone statue. The princesses raised their wands and called: "One, two, three – wake up, dragon!" |
| 16 | Võlusädemeid lendas igale poole. Lohe tegi silmad lahti, lehvitas tiibu ja naeratas: „Tere, printsessid! Kas mängime?” | Magic sparkles flew everywhere. The dragon opened its eyes, flapped its wings and smiled: "Hello, princesses! Shall we play?" |
| 17 | Lohe tõstis printsessid ükshaaval oma pea peale. Ja siis – viuh! – libisesid nad mööda lohe selga ja saba alla nagu liumäest! | The dragon lifted the princesses onto its head one by one. And then – whoosh! – they slid down its back and tail like a slide! |
| 18 | Nii mängisid nad terve pärastlõuna. Üles ja alla, üles ja alla – ikka ja jälle! | They played like that the whole afternoon. Up and down, up and down – again and again! |
| 19 | Õhtu poole oli lohe väga väsinud. Ta haigutas suurelt, keeras end kerra ja jäi magama. „Head ööd, lohe!” sosistasid printsessid. | Towards evening the dragon was very tired. It gave a big yawn, curled up and fell asleep. "Good night, dragon!" whispered the princesses. |
| 20 | Printsessid läksid koju. Fridat ootas õhtusöök: soe kartulipuder ja klaas piima. | The princesses went home. Dinner was waiting for Frida: warm mashed potatoes and a glass of milk. |
| 21 | Siis oli aeg magama minna. Frida pesi hambad puhtaks, pani selga pidžaama ja valis välja unejutu. | Then it was time for bed. Frida brushed her teeth, put on her pyjamas and picked a bedtime story. |
| 22 | Väljas süttisid tähed. Frida kallistas kaisukaru ja sulges silmad. Ka sõbrad ja suur lohe magasid juba. Head ööd! | Outside the stars came out. Frida hugged her teddy bear and closed her eyes. Her friends and the big dragon were already asleep. Good night! |
| 23 | **Lõpp** | The End |

Have a native speaker read it once aloud before printing — especially the rhythm of pages 7 and 8.

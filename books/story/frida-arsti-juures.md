# Printsess Frida arsti juures

The doctor book: a gentle check-up with Doctor Liis (white coat, auburn bob, glasses), meant to make a first visit less scary: waiting room, height and weight, listening to the heart, ears and throat, the knee reflex, checking teddy, one quick injection (no needle drawn; Frida looks at her teddy and holds Ema's hand), a star plaster and stickers. 15 pages.
The source of truth is `web/doctor-story.js` (`text` and `en` per page). Edit there, then update this file.

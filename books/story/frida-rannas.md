# Printsess Frida rannas

The beach book: Frida, Ema, Isa and Uku spend a summer day at the seaside: sun cream first, then a sandcastle (and the wave that flattens it), a crab, swimming with a ring, Uku buried in sand, a cheeky seagull, shells, ice cream and the sunset. 16 pages.
The source of truth is `web/beach-story.js` (`text` and `en` per page). Edit there, then update this file.

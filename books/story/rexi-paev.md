# Väikese Rexi päev

The dinosaur book: same rhythm as *Printsess Frida päev* (wake up, play, lunch, nap, the big friend, evening, sleep), same length (24 pages).
The source of truth is `web/dino-story.js` (`text` and `en` per page). Edit there, then update this file.

| # | Eesti keeles | English |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Väikese Rexi päev** | Little Rex’s Day |
| 1 | Hommikul paistis päike koopasse. Väike T-rex Rex ärkas üles, sirutas oma lühikesi käsi ja haigutas: „Tere hommikust!” | In the morning the sun shone into the cave. Little T-rex Rex woke up, stretched his short arms and yawned: “Good morning!” |
| 2 | Rex pesi hambaid. Tal oli nii palju hambaid! Üleval ja all, vasakul ja paremal – kuni kõik hambad särasid! | Rex brushed his teeth. He had so many teeth! Top and bottom, left and right – until all his teeth sparkled! |
| 3 | Hommikusöögiks sõi Rex suurelt lehelt magusaid marju. Amps-amps! Kõht sai kenasti täis. | For breakfast Rex ate sweet berries from a big leaf. Chomp-chomp! His tummy was nicely full. |
| 4 | Siis luges Rex raamatut vulkaanidest. Kõige naljakam pilt oli munast kooruvast dinobeebist. | Then Rex read a book about volcanoes. The funniest picture was of a baby dino hatching from an egg. |
| 5 | Koopa eest kostis: „Rex, tule mängima!” Seal ootasid sõbrad: triceratops Tipi, stegosaurus Stella, ankülosaurus Anki ja lendsisalik Pteri. | From outside the cave came a call: “Rex, come and play!” His friends were waiting: Tipi the triceratops, Stella the stegosaurus, Anki the ankylosaurus and Pteri the pterosaur. |
| 6 | Dinosaurused mängisid palli. Tipi puksis palli sarvedega ja Anki lõi seda oma raske sabaga. Põmm! | The dinosaurs played ball. Tipi butted the ball with her horns and Anki hit it with his heavy tail. Boom! |
| 7 | Siis leidsid nad suured porilombid. Kõik hüppasid sisse – lirts ja lärts! Pori lendas igale poole. | Then they found big mud puddles. Everyone jumped in – splish and splash! Mud flew everywhere. |
| 8 | Nüüd mängiti peitust. Kõik peitsid end sõnajalgade taha. Aga Stella seljaplaadid paistsid välja! „Leidsin!” hüüdis Rex. | Now they played hide-and-seek. Everyone hid behind the ferns. But Stella’s back plates stuck out! “Found you!” shouted Rex. |
| 9 | Suure puu otsas kasvasid punased õunad. Rex ei ulatunud, aga Pteri lendas üles ja viskas kõigile õuna. Krõmps! | Red apples grew high up in a big tree. Rex couldn’t reach, but Pteri flew up and threw everyone an apple. Crunch! |
| 10 | Varsti oli lõunaaeg. Sõbrad lehvitasid üksteisele: „Head aega! Homme mängime jälle!” Ja kõik läksid koju. | Soon it was lunchtime. The friends waved: “Goodbye! Let’s play again tomorrow!” And everyone went home. |
| 11 | Kodus ootas Rexi lõunasöök: porgandid ja brokoli. Krõmps-krõmps! „Aitäh, oli väga maitsev!” | At home lunch was waiting for Rex: carrots and broccoli. Crunch-crunch! “Thank you, it was delicious!” |
| 12 | Enne lõunauinakut tegi Rex oma pesa lehtedest pehmeks ja võttis kaissu oma kaisumuna. | Before his nap Rex made his nest soft with leaves and hugged his cuddly egg. |
| 13 | Rex keeras end pesas kerra ja sulges silmad. Ka Tipi, Stella, Anki ja Pteri magasid oma pesades. Magusat und! | Rex curled up in his nest and closed his eyes. Tipi, Stella, Anki and Pteri slept in their nests too. Sweet dreams! |
| 14 | Pärast uinakut olid kõik jälle reipad. Sõbrad jooksid üle heinamaa järve äärde – trampsti, trampsti! | After the nap everyone was full of energy again. The friends ran across the meadow to the lake – stomp, stomp! |
| 15 | Järve ääres magas nende suur sõber, brahhiosaurus Bruuno. Sõbrad hüüdsid koos: „Üks, kaks, kolm – ärka üles, Bruuno!” | By the lake slept their big friend, Bruuno the brachiosaurus. The friends called together: “One, two, three – wake up, Bruuno!” |
| 16 | Bruuno tegi silmad lahti, sirutas oma pikka kaela ja naeratas: „Tere, väikesed! Kas mängime?” | Bruuno opened his eyes, stretched his long neck and smiled: “Hello, little ones! Shall we play?” |
| 17 | Bruuno tõstis sõbrad ükshaaval oma pea peale. Ja siis – viuh! – lasid nad liugu mööda tema pikka kaela, selga ja saba alla! | Bruuno lifted the friends onto his head one by one. And then – whoosh! – they slid down his long neck, back and tail! |
| 18 | Pteri lendas kõrval ja hõiskas. Nii mängisid nad terve pärastlõuna – üles ja alla, ikka ja jälle! | Pteri flew alongside, cheering. They played like that the whole afternoon – up and down, again and again! |
| 19 | Õhtu poole oli Bruuno väga väsinud. Ta haigutas suurelt, pani pea murule ja jäi magama. „Head ööd, Bruuno!” sosistasid sõbrad. | Towards evening Bruuno was very tired. He yawned a big yawn, laid his head on the grass and fell asleep. “Good night, Bruuno!” whispered the friends. |
| 20 | Rex läks koju. Õhtusöögiks oli soe juurviljasupp. Lürps! | Rex went home. For dinner there was warm vegetable soup. Slurp! |
| 21 | Siis oli aeg magama minna. Rex pesi hambad – kõik need paljud hambad! – ja valis välja unejutu. | Then it was time for bed. Rex brushed his teeth – all those many teeth! – and picked a bedtime story. |
| 22 | Väljas süttisid tähed. Rex kallistas oma kaisumuna ja sulges silmad. Ka sõbrad ja suur Bruuno magasid juba. Head ööd! | Outside the stars came out. Rex hugged his cuddly egg and closed his eyes. His friends and big Bruuno were already asleep. Good night! |
| 23 | **Lõpp** | The End |

Have a native speaker read it aloud once, especially the names of the dinosaurs (*triceratops, stegosaurus, ankülosaurus, brahhiosaurus*) and *lendsisalik* (pterosaur, which is not technically a dinosaur).

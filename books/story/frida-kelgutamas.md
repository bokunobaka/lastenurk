# Printsess Frida kelgutamas

The winter book: a snowy morning, getting dressed warmly (Uku looks like a ball), crunchy snow, a snowman wearing Ema's scarf, the sledding hill, tipping over, pulling the sled back up, snow angels, a snowball fight and warm cocoa. 14 pages.
The source of truth is `web/winter-story.js` (`text` and `en` per page). Edit there, then update this file.

| 1 | Hommikul vaatas Frida aknast välja. Kõik oli valge! Öösel oli sadanud palju-palju lund. | In the morning Frida looked out of the window. Everything was white! Lots and lots of snow had fallen in the night. |
| 2 | Enne õue minekut pandi selga soojad riided: kombe, müts, sall, labakindad ja saapad. Uku mähiti nii paksult, et ta nägi välja nagu pall! | Before going out they put on warm clothes: snowsuit, hat, scarf, mittens and boots. Uku was wrapped up so thick that he looked like a ball! |

| 5 | Siis läksid nad suurele kelgumäele. Frida ja isa istusid kelgule ja – viuh! – sõitsid alla nagu tuul! | Then they went to the big sledding hill. Frida and Dad sat on the sled and – whoosh! – raced down like the wind! |

| 9 | Isa viskas pehme lumepalli – plärts! Frida viskas vastu. Algas suur ja lõbus lumesõda! | Dad threw a soft snowball – splat! Frida threw one back. A big, fun snowball fight began! |

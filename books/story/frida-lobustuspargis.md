# Printsess Frida lõbustuspargis

The funfair book: carousel (Frida on a horse, Uku on a lion), teacups, bumper cars, duck fishing (Uku wins a teddy), candyfloss, the big wheel, a little rollercoaster (Uku is too small and watches with Ema), the house of mirrors, balloons and the evening lights. 15 pages.
The source of truth is `web/funfair-story.js` (`text` and `en` per page). Edit there, then update this file.

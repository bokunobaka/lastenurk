# Printsess Frida loomaaias

The zoo book: Frida spends a day at the zoo with Mum (ema), Dad (isa) and her little brother Uku (about 3).
They look at the wild animals (giraffe, elephant, lion, monkeys, penguins, zebras) and pet the gentle ones in
the children's zoo (goats, rabbits, a pony ride, a tortoise), then wash their hands. The lion page teaches that
wild animals are only looked at, never petted. 21 pages.
The source of truth is `web/zoo-story.js` (`text` and `en` per page). Edit there, then update this file.

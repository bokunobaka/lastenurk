# Dinosaur character bible (Väikese Rexi päev)

Use the same **style anchor** as `characters.md`, then make one character sheet per dinosaur and reuse it on every page.
All the dinosaurs are round, soft and smiling, with big kind eyes and rosy cheeks. None of them is scary.

| Name | Species | Colours | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rex** (main) | T-rex | leaf green, cream-yellow striped belly, darker green spots and back spikes | big head, **tiny arms**, a cheeky grin with lots of small white teeth |
| **Tipi** | triceratops | sky blue, pink frill with cream dots, cream horns | three horns, small beak |
| **Stella** | stegosaurus | lilac purple, pink and light-pink back plates | small head, spiky tail |
| **Anki** | ankylosaurus | orange with darker orange armour and light studs | low and wide, **club tail** |
| **Pteri** | pteranodon (lendsisalik) | pink, light-pink wings, yellow beak | long crest, always flying |
| **Bruuno** | brachiosaurus | warm yellow with orange spots, cream belly | huge and gentle, very long neck and tail; lies on the grass like a slide |

Props: Rex's **cuddly egg** (cream with blue, pink and yellow spots), a leaf nest, a stone table and a glowing mushroom lamp in the cave.

## Character-sheet prompt (example)

> [style anchor] Character turnaround sheet of a cute little cartoon T-rex named Rex for a 5-year-old's picture book:
> leaf-green, cream-yellow striped belly, darker green spots, tiny arms, big round head, big kind eyes, rosy cheeks,
> cheeky grin showing many small white teeth. Front, side, back and three-quarter views, plus expressions
> (happy, yawning, sleeping, roaring). White background, consistent proportions.

Swap in the details from the table for Tipi, Stella, Anki, Pteri and Bruuno.
For page prompts, follow `art/scenes.md` and use the scene descriptions in `story/rexi-paev.md`.
